Is the first author's record right? · author check

How to judge (click to fold)

100 people drawn at random (seed 20261019) from the 1,234 first authors of the modelled papers of the 2026-10-19 sample. Each card shows the record the citation model uses for that person: the papers counted by the ADS query, refereed and in astronomy. Open "ADS search of the counted papers" (oldest first) and judge whether every counted paper is this person's. The search runs on today's ADS, so it can show a few papers added since the freeze; the papers-per-year line is the count of the freeze.

Right record
The counted papers are this person's. For a shared name some of their papers are missing by design (the count is a lower limit): that is not an error.
Wrong record
Papers of somebody else are counted: an identical name of another person (often an old, unrelated record), or spellings ADS attaches to this person that are someone else. Say which in the note.
Unsure
Cannot tell from the ADS record and the ORCID.
